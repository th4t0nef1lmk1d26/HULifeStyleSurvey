HU Lifestyle Survey

This project is a survey that was designed to find out if residents in the same hall share the same opinions on HU topics.

Questions were asked such as:

  • "How many events do you participate in?"
  • "How often do you eat breakfast?"
  • "How many steps do you average per day?"

We'll be moving through the project to see how these questions stacked up.
