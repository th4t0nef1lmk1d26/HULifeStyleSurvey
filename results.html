HU Lifestyle Survey

Results & Limitaitons

First, there were several limitations my survey encountered.

  • The survey was only spread through my phone and through the moodle page, meaning that wherever I am is likely where the survey takers also are often; questions like "Where do you study most?" are going to be biased in their answers.
  • I should have included which floor of which hall you are on, since different floors seem to have vastly different lifestyles here on campus.
  • Some floors simply recieved more answers than others, providing less than ideal test results.

My findings were largely inconclusive, but there were some fun observations to collect.

Firstly, as you probably noticed on the stepcount graph, there were some big outliers whenever I let users input their own answers. I decided I had to clean up some of the data because someone inputed "200 steps a day" and someone else inputed "81730", which was more than likely not accurate.

Aside from that, event participation seems consistant among most halls while the average step count seems to have some patterns. Sister halls Baker and Roush have a similar and lower average than other halls, likely due to them being closer to classes and having a more secluded style (50% of Roush responders prefer to study in their rooms, and 57% of Baker prefers their room or the quiet space of the library).

Conclusion

Overall, while there are definitely similarites between dorms I believe it is ultimately a student's major which determines their lifestyle and the people they relate to. The people they relate to are then likely to be their dormmates and share the same lifestyle.
